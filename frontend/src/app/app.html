Good to know

A research prototype,
not a monitoring system.

01

One input format

The form and the CSV upload both use raw NSL-KDD features. Encoding, scaling and feature engineering happen in the backend.

02

Supplied records only

It classifies the records you provide. It does not capture packets, connect to a network or block traffic.

03

Know the dataset

NSL-KDD is an older benchmark. High scores on it are not proof of detecting modern attacks.
